# Surface brief: Sub-Sub, one world across the website, tutorial, workshop and browser view

Scope: the website home (site/src/index.njk) proves the world first, in Persuade mode; the rest of the site and the tutorial are Read; the workshop is a slide deck (Experience); the browser view (web/) is Operate. One world, four registers.

Audience and job: students and clinical researchers, equally. The visitor must believe within seconds that Sub-Sub shows its evidence. Primary action: install (the command with its OS tabs). Failures to avoid: generic SaaS, preciousness.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every claim is a figure. The product is shown the way an 1839 natural-history plate shows a specimen: numbered figures, leader lines, captions. Refuses the category's split hero (headline beside a screenshot, feature cards below).

OWN-WORLD: Engraved plates: a cool grey-green sheet (#e9ece6) with a lighter plate field inside a plate mark; copperplate blue-black ink (#16202a) for type and fine engraved rules; transparent hand-coloured washes as the only colour: Prussian blue (Researcher), madder (Librarian), sap green (applied, verified), gamboge (preview). Dark mode is the steel plate itself: blue-black ground, pale engraved line. A serif with a true italic for captions and headings; a hyperlegible sans for controls; small caps for plate and figure numbers. State is drawn by line: solid (full text, applied), dashed (abstract, preview), dotted (metadata), struck (undone).

STORY: The visitor sees the tool at work, labelled part by part (the preview, the evidence line, the checked quote with its page, Undo, the mode), reads the caption, understands that every claim can be checked, and copies the install command.

FIRST VIEWPORT: PLATE I fills the viewport inside a plate mark. Running head: SUB-SUB left, PLATE I. right. Left column (about 2/5): the legend: name, the one-line claim, the install command with OS tabs (primary action), the free-model note. Right (3/5): the browser view as the specimen at large scale, Fig. 1 to Fig. 5 numbered in the margins with leader lines to their parts. Below, the italic caption explaining the figures. Signature interaction: pointing at a figure number (or its caption entry) draws its leader line and inks its part while the rest of the plate rests in plate tone; keyboard and touch work the same; reduced motion shows the lines without drawing.

FORM: The Natural-History Plate (Beale 1839, Scoresby 1820), position 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key dae402a2. Raised by the declined hand: state drawn by line, not colour alone (jackfield); live rows that change in place (split-flap); one linear install sequence (installer); processes as stages with a still record (raku); every figure with a text equivalent (Miura); only the figure in focus fully inked (cascade).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: the serif face (chosen from a specimen against the brief: true italic, period resonance, screen reading); the reading mode for long notes (browser view and tutorial).
