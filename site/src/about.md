## The name

Moby-Dick opens with the Extracts: quotations about whales, "supplied by a Sub-Sub-Librarian", a poor devil who "appears to have gone through the long Vaticans and street-stalls of the earth, picking up whatever random allusions to whales he could anyways find in any book whatsoever". Sub-Sub does the same for your library, with less despair.

## The author

Sub-Sub is made by Tiago Jacinto, assistant professor at the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Porto (MEDCIDS, RISE-Health), for his own library first and then for students and colleagues. Contact: [tiagojacinto@med.up.pt](mailto:tiagojacinto@med.up.pt).

## Built with

- [pi](https://pi.dev), the agent that Sub-Sub extends.
- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org) and its local API.
