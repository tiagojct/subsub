New to Sub-Sub? [Para estudantes](/pt/estudantes/) explains it in Portuguese, from the start: what it does, what it does not do, and how to start for free.

## Set up

1. [Install Sub-Sub](/install/).
2. When `subsub init` asks for the set-up, select "FMUP / U.Porto".

This gives you a starter tag list for the health sciences: clinical areas, public health, methods and study designs.

To learn to read papers, use `/read` and a citekey. Sub-Sub finds the passages and asks questions, and you write the answers. `/lit reading list:` and a topic gives a reading list with the screening log: the works to read and questions to read them with. See [Learn to read the literature](/profiles/#learn-to-read-the-literature).

## Papers that are not open access

Sub-Sub finds open-access copies of papers. For a paper that is not open access:

1. Use Sub-Sub on the U.Porto network. There, the DOI link opens the publisher's version through the library's subscriptions.
2. If the library does not subscribe to the journal, type `/request-copy` and the citekey, DOI or PMID. Sub-Sub drafts an email to the corresponding author, with the address from the PubMed record. You read the draft and send it yourself.

Sub-Sub does not use unlicensed sources.

## The FMUP model service

Soon, the FMUP set-up will include a model service at no cost to you. Until then, connect your own provider with the model button (in the terminal, `/login`; see [Models](/models/)).

## Rules for the use of AI

Sub-Sub helps you find, organise and read the literature. It does not draft text for assignments, even when you ask. The rules of your course and of the faculty about AI apply to what you hand in.

[FMUP · IA](https://tiagojacinto.eu/fmup-ia/) is a proposed reference framework for the use of generative AI at FMUP, with Themis, a tool that helps you write a declaration of AI use. It is a proposal for institutional review, not approved faculty policy.
